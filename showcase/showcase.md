# Every angle is an opportunity.

Carom is a quick paddle duel with a twist in every return. Take the mint paddle against the computer, or bring a friend to the coral side for a local two-player match. A brief serve gives you time to set your position. Then the field comes alive.

Meet the ball while sliding and your movement puts spin into its flight. Watch the trail bend as a straight return becomes a curling shot. Catch the ball near an edge to send it high or low; stay still to keep the exchange controlled. Every paddle hit makes the rally faster.

Two amber obstacles split the court into unexpected routes. Bank a shot around them, redirect it off a wall, or use a narrow opening to catch your opponent moving the wrong way. The same layout keeps revealing new angles as the pace rises.

The first player to eleven wins, but only with a two-point lead. At ten-all, every save buys another chance and every missed return brings match point closer. Find your line, time your slide, and make the next shot yours.
